EARNMAX PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: 26 October 2026

1. About this app
Earnmax is a rewards app. You watch ads, earn coins, and request a withdrawal to your UPI ID once you reach a withdrawal tier. This policy explains what information the app handles.

2. Account information
When you sign up, you enter your name, email address, and a password. In the current version of the app, this information is stored only on your own device. We do not run a server that collects or stores your account in this version. If a future version adds online accounts, this policy will be updated before that change.

3. Coins, bonuses, and referrals
Your coins, bonus coins, referral code, and withdrawal history are also stored only on your device in the current version. Your referral code is created by you and can be shared by you with people you invite.

4. Withdrawal requests
When you request a withdrawal, you provide a UPI ID. That UPI ID is used only to process your payout request. Withdrawals are reviewed manually before payment, so payouts are not instant.

5. Advertising
The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information and advertising identifiers to serve ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. What we do not collect
The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files. It does not access anything on your device beyond what is needed to run the app and show ads.

7. Children
Earnmax is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app.

8. Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
